Vehicle Service Management Mobile Application

Backend: Python Django
Database: SQLite3
Frontend: Flutter (Mobile Application)

📌 Application Overview

This Vehicle Service Application connects:

Vehicle Owners (Users)

Service Center Owners

Mechanics

The system allows users to search nearby service centers, book vehicle services, track service status, and manage service history. Service centers manage bookings and mechanics. Mechanics update real-time service progress.

The design follows real-world workshop workflow logic.

🏗️ Required Portals

User Portal (Vehicle Owner)

Service Center Owner Portal

Mechanic Portal

All portals are mobile-based with role-based login redirection.

🔄 Real-World Workflow Logic

User searches and books appointment.

Service center owner reviews and confirms booking.

On service day, vehicle received and status updated.

Mechanic performs inspection and updates job.

Owner updates cost and seeks user approval if needed.

Mechanic completes service.

Owner generates final bill.

User makes payment.

Service marked completed.

User leaves review.
